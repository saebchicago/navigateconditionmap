# Landscape notes: where heart failure, type 2 diabetes, and CKD care is heading

Compiled Oct 29, 2026 for the ConditionMap and Navigator roadmap. These notes are context for planning. Navigator does not use them at runtime and its cards are unchanged by them except as listed in the README change log.

Labels: VERIFIED = read from a primary source (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, a CDC page). UNCERTAIN = from search snippets or secondary summaries.

## Trials behind two evidence cards (VERIFIED, PubMed)

- FLOW (Perkovic, NEJM 2024;391:109-121): 3,533 adults with type 2 diabetes and CKD, semaglutide 1.0 mg weekly vs placebo, median 3.4 years. Primary outcome (kidney failure, at least 50% eGFR drop, or death from kidney or cardiovascular causes) was 24% lower (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.66 to 0.88).
- CONFIDENCE (Agarwal, NEJM 2025;393:533-543): phase 2, Bayer-funded, 180 days. The combination of finerenone and empagliflozin lowered UACR 29% more than finerenone alone and 32% more than empagliflozin alone. No unexpected adverse events. It measured a biomarker, not kidney failure or death.

## Guideline direction (UNCERTAIN, secondary reports)

- ESC 2026 heart failure guideline reported published Aug 28, 2026; SGLT2 inhibitors and MRAs described as foundational across ejection fraction. Read the guideline text before changing the ESC-based cards.
- KDIGO diabetes-in-CKD 2026 draft was open for public review until Apr 13, 2026. No final publication found.
- A 2026 AHA/ACC/ADA/ASN cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic guideline is reported as published June 2026 (listing only).
- No new AHA/ACC/HFSA heart failure management guideline found.

## Trial pipeline (VERIFIED, ClinicalTrials.gov, recruiting phase 3, queried Oct 29, 2026; broad queries include off-target trials)

CKD 45, heart failure 65, type 2 diabetes 73, finerenone 9. Examples: REDEFINE-HF (NCT06008197, finerenone, EF 40% or above, primary completion 2027-11); FINALITY-HF (NCT06033950, primary completion 2026-08); MARITIME-HF (NCT07037459, 2028-06).

## Coverage and cost (UNCERTAIN, secondary)

- Medicare negotiated prices in effect since Jan 1, 2026 include Farxiga and Entresto. Semaglutide's negotiated price takes effect Jan 1, 2027. Trulicity is in the round effective 2028. A Medicare GLP-1 obesity Bridge program started Jul 1, 2026. Confirm all of this against CMS before quoting.

## Epidemiology (VERIFIED, CDC pages unless noted)

- CKD: about 14% of US adults (about 37 million), about 87% unaware (CDC, Mar 31, 2026).
- Diabetes: 40.1 million (12.0%) in 2023; 27.6% of adults with diabetes undiagnosed (CDC, Sep 16, 2026).
- Heart failure: about 7.7 million adults (UNCERTAIN, secondary summary of HFSA 2026 statistics).
